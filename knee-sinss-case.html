The Knee That Didn’t Bounce Back

You are the PTA carrying out an established PT Plan of Care. Review the note, interview the patient, determine SINSS, then choose appropriate interventions.

PT Plan of Care Note

Diagnosis

Reactive patellar tendinopathy of the right knee.

Mechanism of Injury

Patient reports onset of anterior knee pain after increasing running mileage and adding downhill routes. Symptoms began after a 3-mile run with stair descents.

Date of Injury

Symptoms began approximately 3 weeks ago.

Subjective

• Current pain: 6/10
• Best: 2/10
• Worst: 7/10
• Pain pattern: intermittent anterior knee pain
• 24-hour pattern: worse with activity, mild morning stiffness
• Stability: symptoms unchanged over past week
• Previous activity: running 3–4 miles, 3x/week
• Current limitations: stairs, squatting, prolonged standing
• Patient goals: return to running without pain

Objective

ROM (AROM/PROM):
• R knee flexion AROM: 130° (pain end range)
• R knee extension AROM: 0°
• L knee flexion AROM: 140°
• L knee extension AROM: 0°

Strength (MMT):
• R quad: 4/5 (pain)
• L quad: 5/5
• R hip abductors: 4-/5
• L hip abductors: 4+/5

Accessory Motion:
• Patellar superior glide: hypomobile and painful

Flexibility / MLT:
• Quadriceps: mild tightness R>L

Movement Analysis:
• Dynamic valgus during squat
• Pain at ~45° knee flexion

Palpation:
• Tender patellar tendon
• Mild swelling

Special Tests:
• Valgus stress test: negative bilaterally
• Varus stress test: negative bilaterally
• Lachman test: negative bilaterally
• Anterior drawer: negative bilaterally
• Patellar compression test: positive (pain reproduced with compression during quad contraction)

Other:
• Pain descending stairs: 7/10
• Pain during running: 6/10 after 0.5 miles
• Running tolerance: 0.5 miles

Assessment

Reactive patellar tendinopathy with quad weakness and hip control deficits.

Plan

• Isometric quad loading
• Hip strengthening
• Patellar tendon unloading strategies
• Activity modification
• Manual therapy PRN
• Progress to isotonic loading as tolerated
